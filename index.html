How does an NFC business card work?+

The card stores a link to your digital profile. When a compatible phone reads the card, it offers to open that link. Your contact details and other content live on the online profile.

Will it work with iPhone and Android?+

It depends on the phone and its NFC support. Supported iPhones can read website tags in the background; Android behaviour varies by device and settings. The QR code is another way to open the same profile. We’ll help you check compatibility before you order.

Apple’s NFC guidance ↗
Does the person need a special app?+

They can open the profile in their web browser. Supported phones can read the NFC website link without a separate card app. If tapping isn’t supported, they can use their phone’s QR scanning feature.

Can I change my details later?+

Yes. We can update the content on your online profile while keeping its link the same. You don’t need a new card for a new phone number or social link. Ask us about the update and support arrangements when we set it up.

Does it need internet or a battery?+

The NFC card itself doesn’t need a battery. The receiving phone needs an internet connection to load your online profile. Saving the contact is a separate action chosen by the recipient.

Can you make cards for our whole team?+

Tell us your team size and branding needs. We can plan a consistent card design and an individual digital profile for each person. Card availability, production, support, and delivery are confirmed with you before ordering.
